Ask Me Agent.

A smart food app: scan a food product and it tells you, in plain words, whether it’s right for you and your family, and answers anything else you want to ask about it.

Product
Smart food app, iOS
Focus
AI agent, personalization, nutrition
Problem

The problem

A food label lists nutrients, but it doesn’t say whether the product is right for you. Shoppers had to read, decode and judge every pack themselves, for themselves and for everyone they shop for.

The brief: a mobile app that scans a food product and gives its nutrition detail in a way that actually helps.

The questionHow will a user identify the right product, one that fits their nutrition goals?
Ask me

Ask & learn

Don’t just access information, learn from it. Ask me lets a shopper ask anything about the product in their hand, by voice or text, and answers for them and their family.

  1. Ask in your own words

    Type a question or speak into the microphone, right from the product page. No search terms, no menus.

  2. Answers about you, not the average

    Answers draw on the goals and people set up at the start: not “high in calcium”, but “15% more calcium than average, good for Raju’s growth”.

  3. Learn more than the label

    Every answer can go deeper, with a follow-up question or a short read, so shoppers understand the benefit and trust the choice.

Outcome

Outcome

Shoppers stop decoding labels: a product becomes an answer about them.

Answers, not data

Ask me turns nutrition facts into a plain answer to the shopper’s own question.

About you, not the average

Every answer is for the people the shopper buys for, not for a label’s average adult.

Understood, not just told

Every answer can go deeper, so shoppers know why a product suits them and trust their choice.
